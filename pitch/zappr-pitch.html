the problem
The problem

Bitcoin works. The experience doesn't.

A Lagos merchant, paid in Bitcoin for the first time, finally got it to land —
looked up and said: "this is supposed to be the future?"

It took three apps, a YouTube tutorial, and a WhatsApp helper sending links she couldn't read. The payment worked. The experience didn't. That sentence is why zappr exists.

Fragmented

Wallet, social, and help live in three separate apps with zero memory between them.

English-only

Every Bitcoin tool ships in English first. A Yoruba trader must think in a second language to send money.

Jargon wall

Lightning, NWC, zaps, nsec — the vocabulary alone filters out most would-be users.

False choice

"Easy" apps custody your keys. Sovereign apps are too technical. Nothing is both.

Our solution

zappr — one surface, every language.

A self-custodial Bitcoin social + payments hub that puts your Nostr feed, a Lightning wallet, and a Bitcoin-native AI guide on one screen — and lets the guide speak whatever language you walked in with.

how it works
The tech + the plan

Sovereign tech. A real path to scale.

The technology

Nostr · NDK

Identity, follows, relays. We never see your nsec.

Breez SDK

Lightning + Liquid in-browser via WASM. Seedless wallets from a passkey.

MavaPay

Lightning ↔ NGN bank rail. Cash out live on staging.

Claude Agent

Reads, drafts, explains, confirms payments — multilingual. Only AI calls hit our server.

The business plan

Go-to-market

Beachhead: Lagos creators & merchants already on Nostr. The product is its own distribution — every zap is a referral. We win on language; Bitnob & Yellow Card ship English-first.

Monetization

Free & self-custodial. A transparent ~1% ramp spread — Machankura's rate, far under the 8% remittance norm — plus merchant tools & named Lightning addresses. We earn when you transact, never by holding funds.

Scale

Client-side = near-zero cost per user; only AI & ramp liquidity vary. New language = a prompt. The same rails reach a $23B/yr Nigeria remittance corridor, then KES/GHS/ZAR.

Obstacles

Browser gaps → nsec/NIP-07/NWC fallbacks shipped. Fiat & CBN rules → licensed partner (MavaPay), region by region. Ramp liquidity → partner float. Trust → we never custody funds.

Sources: Machankura 1% ramp fee (TechCabal, 2026) · Nigeria spreads 0.5–3% (Breet, 2026) · Nigeria remittances ~$23B in 2025 (Nairametrics / CBN). Target model — offramp currently on staging.

why it matters
Why it matters

When the experience disappears,
Bitcoin becomes infrastructure.

The technology to give 1.4 billion people sovereign money already exists. The only thing standing between them and it is the experience. zappr removes that wall — in their language, on the phone they already own, without ever taking their keys.

Sovereignty

Self-custody that a first-timer can actually use. No seed phrase to lose, no custodian to trust.

Inclusion

Your language, your phone. The 95% that jargon filtered out are finally invited in.

Real economy

Creators get tipped. Merchants cash out to NGN. Money moves at 0.1%, not 8%.

The merchant from slide two? She onboards in one tap — and never has to ask "is this the future?" again.
